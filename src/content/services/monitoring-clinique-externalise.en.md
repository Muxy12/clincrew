Outsourced clinical monitoring means entrusting all or part of a study's monitoring activities to a freelance Clinical Research Associate (CRA), rather than hiring or mobilising an internal team. You keep oversight; I take care of the operational follow-up of the sites assigned to me, in line with your procedures and Good Clinical Practice (ICH E6).

## What an outsourced assignment covers

- Site visits: selection, initiation, monitoring and close-out, on-site or remote
- Site follow-up between visits: reminders, deviation resolution, action tracking
- Verification of data, informed consent and investigational product management
- Reporting: visit reports, open items and corrective actions, in the format and at the frequency agreed

The exact scope (sites, visit types, responsibilities, system access, deliverables) is defined with you before the start, then tracked throughout the assignment.

## When outsourcing makes sense

Entrusting monitoring to a freelance CRA answers concrete situations: a workload growing faster than the team, a study starting before headcount is complete, an absence to cover, or a trial run without a permanent monitoring team. Outsourcing provides an identified resource, available on a precise scope, without recruitment.
