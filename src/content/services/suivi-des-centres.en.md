Investigator site management is everything that happens between visits. A site does not only move forward on monitoring days: it moves forward when someone keeps in touch, follows up at the right time and unblocks points before they become problems. It is a hands-on role, in the spirit of Good Clinical Practice (ICH E6).

## What site management covers

- Regular check-ins with the team: recruitment, enrolment, difficulties encountered
- Resolving queries and tracking corrective actions through to closure
- Anticipating amendments, product shortages or logistical constraints
- Connecting the site with the rest of the study (sponsor, data management, pharmacy)
- Preparing upcoming visits so they are useful rather than redundant

## Why continuity matters

A site left on its own between visits drifts quietly: queries pile up, recruitment stalls, deviations go unnoticed. A regular presence, even remote, keeps the momentum and avoids nasty surprises at the next visit.
