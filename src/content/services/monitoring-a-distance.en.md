Remote monitoring covers the follow-up activities carried out without travelling to the site, where the protocol, procedures and authorised access allow. It does not replace on-site visits: it ensures continuity between them and makes it possible to react earlier, within a risk-based monitoring approach (ICH E6).

## What I handle remotely

- Review of data entered in the EDC and follow-up of queries
- Tracking of open items and corrective actions arising from visits
- Regular exchanges with site teams: recruitment, difficulties, protocol questions
- Preparation of on-site visits, so they focus on what genuinely requires being on site

## The conditions required

Remote monitoring operates within a strict framework: authorised access to systems, respect for the confidentiality of participant data, and activities explicitly planned in the monitoring plan. Source data verification against original documents remains, unless otherwise specified, carried out on site.

## Alongside on-site visits

I combine remote follow-up and on-site visits according to the risk level of each site and each data point. The goal is simple: keep every site active and up to date between visits, and prevent deviations from building up until the next one.
