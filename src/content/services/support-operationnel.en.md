Clinical operations support is flexible backup, available when your team needs it. A workload peak, a departure, a difficult site to take over, a study starting faster than planned: rather than hiring in a hurry, you add senior capacity that is already operational, in line with Good Clinical Practice (ICH E6).

## When I step in

- Monitoring backup during a busy period or an enrolment peak
- Taking over sites left without follow-up (turnover, team overload)
- Operational coordination and interface between sites, sponsor and vendors
- Ad-hoc support on a start-up, a close-out or a data catch-up
- Cover during an absence or a transition phase

## Why flexibility is an asset

A study's needs are not linear. Freelance backup adjusts to reality: it arrives quickly, focuses on what is blocking, and steps back when the load eases — without the commitment of a permanent hire. It is capacity when you need it, where you need it.
