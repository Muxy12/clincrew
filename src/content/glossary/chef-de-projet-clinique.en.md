The clinical project manager (CPM) steers a clinical trial as a whole: budget, timelines, teams, sites and vendors. Where the CRA acts at site level, the project manager coordinates the entire study on behalf of the sponsor or the CRO.

## What a project manager steers

- The schedule, budget and resources of the study
- The coordination of [CRAs](/en/blog/qu-est-ce-qu-un-arc-cra), sites and vendors
- Reporting to the sponsor and risk tracking

## Project manager or freelance CRA?

The two roles are complementary: one steers, the other delivers in the field. To choose according to your need, see [freelance CRA vs clinical project manager](/en/blog/arc-freelance-vs-chef-de-projet-clinique).
