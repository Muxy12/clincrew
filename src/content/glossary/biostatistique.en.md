Biostatistics applies statistical methods to clinical research. It comes into play from the design of the trial (calculating the number of participants needed) to the analysis and interpretation of the results. It ensures a trial's conclusions rest on solid foundations.

## What biostatistics covers

- Sample size calculation and power
- The statistical analysis plan
- The analysis of endpoints and interpretation

## Biostatistics and data

The biostatistician works with the [data manager](/en/glossaire/data-manager) and relies on reliable data, notably from [randomisation](/en/glossaire/randomisation) and rigorous collection in the field.

*Reference: clinical trial methodology.*
