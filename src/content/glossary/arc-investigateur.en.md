The site CRA (or site-based CRA) works within the healthcare facility, alongside the investigator. They organise the study's logistics at the site: preparing visits, collecting and entering data, and managing documents and participant appointments.

## Their role

- Coordinate the study at the investigator site
- Prepare and enter data in the case report form (eCRF)
- Facilitate the visits of the sponsor CRA and monitors

## An internal position, not external

The site CRA belongs to the site, whereas the [sponsor CRA](/en/glossaire/arc-promoteur) works on behalf of the sponsor. The two collaborate closely during [monitoring visits](/en/services/visites-de-monitoring). At the site, the site CRA often works with a [clinical trial technician](/en/glossaire/tec).

*Reference: ICH E6 (Good Clinical Practice).*
