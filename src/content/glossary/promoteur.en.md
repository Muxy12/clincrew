The sponsor is the person, company or institution that initiates a clinical trial and takes responsibility for it. It may be a pharmaceutical company, a biotech or an academic sponsor (hospital, university). The sponsor defines the protocol, funds the study and keeps responsibility for its conduct and decisions.

## Its responsibilities

- Design the protocol and obtain the authorisations (competent authority, ethics committee)
- Select the sites and the vendors (CRO, CRA)
- Safeguard participant safety and data integrity

## Sponsor and monitoring

The sponsor oversees the study but often delegates monitoring to a [CRO](/en/glossaire/cro) or a CRA. It can also call on a [freelance CRA to support its team](/en/solutions/monitoring-promoteur).

*Reference: ICH E6 (Good Clinical Practice).*
