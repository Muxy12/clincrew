The EMA (European Medicines Agency) is the European Union agency responsible for the scientific evaluation, supervision and safety monitoring of medicines. It plays a central role in marketing authorisation at European level and in the coordination of clinical trials.

## Its role

- Evaluate medicines for their [marketing authorisation](/en/glossaire/amm)
- Coordinate safety monitoring (pharmacovigilance) across the EU
- Support the European clinical trials framework (CTR, CTIS portal)

## EMA and national authorities

The EMA works with national authorities such as [ANSM](/en/glossaire/ansm). Since the [EU Clinical Trials Regulation](/en/glossaire/reglement-europeen-essais-cliniques), the authorisation of multi-country trials has been harmonised.

*Reference: European medicines regulation.*
