The investigator is the physician responsible for conducting a clinical trial at a site. When several people form a site's team, the principal investigator takes responsibility for it. They safeguard participant safety, protocol compliance and the reliability of the data collected at their site.

## Their role

- Run the study at the site according to the protocol and Good Clinical Practice
- Protect participants' rights and safety
- Ensure the accuracy of source data

## Investigator and CRA

The investigator is the key counterpart of the [sponsor CRA](/en/glossaire/arc-promoteur) during [monitoring visits](/en/services/visites-de-monitoring). A relationship of trust with the investigator is decisive for the smooth running of the study.

*Reference: ICH E6 (Good Clinical Practice).*
