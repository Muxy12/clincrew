The monitoring plan is the document that defines how a clinical trial will be monitored: the strategy, the frequency and scope of visits, the data to verify and the risk-based approach. Prepared by the sponsor or the CRO before the start, it frames the CRA's work at each site.

## What a monitoring plan contains

- The monitoring strategy (on-site, remote, centralised) and its intensity
- The frequency and type of visits per site
- The data and documents to verify, including the level of SDV
- The risk criteria and the triggers for adjustment
- The responsibilities, the expected deliverables and their routing

## Why it is central

The monitoring plan turns the risk-based approach of ICH E6 (R2) into concrete actions. It ensures the oversight effort is concentrated where the risk is highest, and that every CRA applies the same logic from one site to the next.
