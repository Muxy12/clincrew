The protocol is the reference document of a clinical trial. It describes the objectives, methodology, eligibility criteria, treatments, assessments and conduct of the study. Everyone involved complies with it: it is the trial's roadmap.

## What a protocol contains

- The objectives and endpoints
- The [inclusion and exclusion criteria](/en/glossaire/criteres-inclusion-exclusion)
- The treatment schedule and assessments
- The statistical and safety aspects

## Protocol and amendment

Any change to the protocol is made through an amendment, submitted to the authorities and the [ethics committee](/en/glossaire/cpp) when required. Compliance with the protocol is at the heart of [monitoring](/en/glossaire/monitoring-clinique) and of the [monitoring plan](/en/glossaire/plan-de-monitoring).

*Reference: ICH E6 (Good Clinical Practice).*
