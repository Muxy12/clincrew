A CRO (Contract Research Organization) is a provider a sponsor entrusts with all or part of running a clinical trial: monitoring, data management, regulatory, biostatistics. It supplies teams, including Clinical Research Associates.

## What a CRO takes on

- Monitoring of investigator sites
- Data management and analysis
- Regulatory and pharmacovigilance activities

## CRO and freelance CRA

A CRO can reinforce its monitoring teams on an occasional basis with a freelance CRA, to absorb a workload peak or cover an absence, without recruitment.
