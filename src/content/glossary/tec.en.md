The clinical trial technician (TEC, from the French "technicien d'études cliniques") assists the investigator within the site. They handle a trial's operational logistics: organising participant visits, collecting and entering data, and tracking documents and samples.

## What a clinical trial technician does

- Schedule participant visits and examinations
- Collect and enter data in the case report form (eCRF)
- Prepare monitoring visits and answer queries

## Technician, CRA, project manager: who does what?

The technician works at the site, like the [site CRA](/en/glossaire/arc-investigateur). They differ from the [sponsor CRA](/en/glossaire/arc-promoteur), who monitors on behalf of the sponsor, and from the [clinical project manager](/en/glossaire/chef-de-projet-clinique), who steers the study as a whole.

*Reference: clinical research, investigator site organisation.*
