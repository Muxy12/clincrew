A clinical trial is a study carried out in humans to evaluate the efficacy and safety of a medicine, a device or a therapeutic strategy. It follows a precise protocol and complies with Good Clinical Practice (ICH E6), which protect participants and ensure the reliability of the results.

## The main principles

- A defined objective and research question
- A protocol framing the methodology and conduct
- The protection of participants (consent, ethics)
- Rigorous and traceable data collection

## How a trial unfolds

A trial is built in [phases](/en/glossaire/phases-essai-clinique) and rests on a [protocol](/en/glossaire/protocole). Its conduct in the field relies on [clinical monitoring](/en/glossaire/monitoring-clinique), carried out by a CRA at investigator sites.

*Reference: ICH E6 (Good Clinical Practice).*
