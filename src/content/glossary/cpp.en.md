The ethics committee, known in France as the CPP (Comité de Protection des Personnes), is an independent body that reviews research projects involving human participants before they start. In France, no clinical trial can begin without its favourable opinion. It protects participants' rights, safety and well-being.

## What the ethics committee assesses

- The protection of participants and informed consent
- The relevance and methodology of the research
- The information given to participants and data handling

## Ethics committee and authorisation

The committee's favourable opinion is required in addition to the [ANSM](/en/glossaire/ansm) authorisation: both are needed. The [sponsor](/en/glossaire/promoteur) submits the dossier before any enrolment.

*Reference: law on research involving human participants.*
