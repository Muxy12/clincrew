Pharmacovigilance is the monitoring, assessment and prevention of medicines' adverse effects. In a clinical trial, it aims to detect any safety signal quickly and to protect participants. It continues after the medicine is marketed.

## What it covers

- The collection and reporting of [adverse events](/en/glossaire/evenement-indesirable)
- The assessment of the link with the medicine and of seriousness
- Regulatory reporting (in Europe, through EudraVigilance)

## Pharmacovigilance and monitoring

The CRA contributes to pharmacovigilance in the field: they check that adverse events are properly documented and reported on time, during [monitoring visits](/en/services/visites-de-monitoring).

*Reference: ICH E6 (Good Clinical Practice).*
