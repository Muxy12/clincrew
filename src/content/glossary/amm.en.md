A marketing authorisation (in French, AMM) is the regulatory authorisation that allows a medicine to be marketed. It is granted by an authority, the [EMA](/en/glossaire/ema) at European level or a national agency such as [ANSM](/en/glossaire/ansm), after assessing the medicine's quality, efficacy and safety, based on the clinical trials.

## What a marketing authorisation attests

- A favourable benefit/risk balance
- Controlled pharmaceutical quality
- Defined conditions of use (indications, dosage)

## Clinical trials and marketing authorisation

Phase I to IV trials build the dossier that supports the marketing authorisation application. After authorisation, [pharmacovigilance](/en/glossaire/pharmacovigilance) continues the safety monitoring.

*Reference: medicines regulation.*
