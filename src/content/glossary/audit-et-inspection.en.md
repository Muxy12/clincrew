Audit and inspection are two forms of compliance control for a clinical trial. The audit is carried out by the sponsor, or on its behalf, independently of the monitoring teams. The inspection is conducted by an authority (ANSM, EMA, FDA). Both verify that the trial complies with the protocol, the procedures and Good Clinical Practice.

## What is checked

- The essential documentation (TMF, investigator site file)
- Source data and its consistency
- Deviations, their management and corrective actions

## Staying ready at all times

The best preparation is a site kept ready at any moment. For the habits to adopt, see [preparing for an audit or inspection](/en/blog/preparer-audit-inspection). The reference framework remains [Good Clinical Practice](/en/glossaire/bonnes-pratiques-cliniques).

*Reference: ICH E6 (Good Clinical Practice).*
