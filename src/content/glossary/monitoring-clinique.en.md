Clinical monitoring is the set of oversight activities on a clinical trial that verify it is conducted in line with the protocol, the procedures and Good Clinical Practice (ICH E6). It protects participant safety and ensures reliable data. Carried out by a Clinical Research Associate (CRA), it takes place at investigator sites, on-site and remotely, throughout the study.

## What monitoring covers

- Protecting participants' rights and safety
- Compliance with the protocol and the regulations
- The quality, completeness and traceability of data
- Regular follow-up of each site and its open items

## A cycle, from activation to close-out

Monitoring follows each site's life: selection visit, initiation visit, monitoring visits, then close-out visit. It relies on a monitoring plan defined upfront and, increasingly, on a risk-based approach (ICH E6 R2): effort is concentrated where the risk to participants and data is highest, through on-site and remote activities.
