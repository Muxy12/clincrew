The sponsor CRA is the Clinical Research Associate who works on behalf of the sponsor or the CRO. They monitor the investigator sites: selection, initiation, monitoring and close-out visits. They ensure protocol compliance, participant safety and data quality, and act as the link between the site and the project team.

## Their role

- Select, initiate, follow up and close out investigator sites
- Verify data, informed consent and investigational product management
- Document deviations and track corrective actions

## Sponsor CRA or site CRA?

Unlike the [site CRA](/en/glossaire/arc-investigateur), who works within the site, the sponsor CRA operates from the outside, on behalf of the sponsor. This is the profile of a freelance CRA reinforcing a CRO or a sponsor.

*Reference: ICH E6 (Good Clinical Practice).*
