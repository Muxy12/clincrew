The EU Clinical Trials Regulation (CTR 536/2014) harmonises the authorisation and conduct of medicine trials across the European Union. It replaced the former directive and relies on a single portal, CTIS (Clinical Trials Information System), to submit and manage multi-country trials.

## What the CTR changed

- A single submission for several countries through CTIS
- A coordinated assessment between member states
- More transparency on trials and their results

## What it means in the field

The framework evolves, but the fundamentals remain: compliance with the protocol, with [Good Clinical Practice](/en/glossaire/bonnes-pratiques-cliniques), the [ethics committee](/en/glossaire/cpp) opinion and the [ANSM](/en/glossaire/ansm) authorisation.

*Reference: Regulation (EU) No 536/2014.*
