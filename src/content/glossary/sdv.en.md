SDV (Source Data Verification) is the activity of comparing the data entered in the case report form (CRF / eCRF) with the source documents at the investigator site (medical record, test results). It is one of the emblematic activities of monitoring: it ensures the study's data faithfully reflect the clinical reality.

## What SDV checks

- The match between source data and the case report form
- The presence and consistency of source documents
- The traceability and dating of data

## Targeted, risk-based verification

ICH E6 (R2) changed SDV: rather than verifying everything everywhere, effort targets the data critical to participant safety and the reliability of results, according to the monitoring plan. SDV now combines with centralised data review and remote monitoring.
