Blinding (or masking) means hiding the information about the treatment received. In single blind, the participant does not know which treatment they receive; in double blind, both the participant and the assessing team do not know. Blinding limits assessment bias, whether conscious or not.

## The levels of blinding

- **Open label**: everyone knows the assigned treatment
- **Single blind**: the participant does not know
- **Double blind**: the participant and the assessors do not know

## Blinding and comparison

Blinding often accompanies [randomisation](/en/glossaire/randomisation) and the use of a [placebo](/en/glossaire/placebo). It protects the objectivity of outcome measurement.
