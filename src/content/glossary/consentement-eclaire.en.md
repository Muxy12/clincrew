Informed consent is the free, voluntary and informed agreement a participant gives before entering a clinical trial. It rests on complete and understandable information: objectives, conduct, expected benefits, risks and participant rights, including the right to withdraw at any time.

## What consent guarantees

- Clear, prior information, free of coercion
- The freedom to accept or refuse, and to withdraw
- A written, dated and signed record

## The CRA's role

During [monitoring visits](/en/services/visites-de-monitoring), the CRA systematically checks the presence, version and dating of consent forms. It is one of the most closely monitored points, because it directly concerns participants' rights, overseen by the [ethics committee](/en/glossaire/cpp).

*Reference: ICH E6 (Good Clinical Practice).*
