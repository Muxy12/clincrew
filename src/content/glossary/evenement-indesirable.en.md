An adverse event (AE) is any untoward medical occurrence in a clinical trial participant, whether or not it is related to the treatment. A serious adverse event (SAE) is an AE that results, for example, in death, hospitalisation, disability or a life-threatening situation.

## The grading of events

- AE: any untoward occurrence, whatever its cause
- SAE: a serious event depending on its consequences
- Causality, and expected or unexpected nature

## The CRA's role

During [monitoring visits](/en/services/visites-de-monitoring), the CRA checks that adverse events are documented, classified and reported on time. It is a central element of [pharmacovigilance](/en/glossaire/pharmacovigilance) and participant safety.

*Reference: ICH E6 (Good Clinical Practice).*
