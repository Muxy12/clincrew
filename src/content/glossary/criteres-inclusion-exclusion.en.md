Inclusion and exclusion criteria define who can take part in a clinical trial. Inclusion criteria are the conditions the participant must meet; exclusion criteria are those that rule them out. Together, they determine eligibility and ensure a consistent and safe study population.

## Why they matter

- Safeguard participant safety
- Ensure the homogeneity of the study population
- Allow a reliable interpretation of the results

## Eligibility and monitoring

Compliance with eligibility criteria is a key point of the [protocol](/en/glossaire/protocole). The CRA checks it during [monitoring visits](/en/services/visites-de-monitoring): an enrolment outside the criteria is a deviation that must be documented and managed.

*Reference: ICH E6 (Good Clinical Practice).*
