ANSM (France's national agency for the safety of medicines and health products) is the French competent authority for medicines. It authorises clinical trials of medicines in France, oversees their safety and can carry out inspections. No medicine trial can start in France without its authorisation.

## Its role for a clinical trial

- Authorise the trial, alongside the European assessment
- Oversee participant safety during the study
- Inspect sponsors, CROs and investigator sites

## ANSM, ethics committee, EMA

A trial in France requires both the ANSM authorisation and the favourable opinion of an [ethics committee](/en/glossaire/cpp). At European level, the assessment is coordinated with the [EMA](/en/glossaire/ema) under the [EU Clinical Trials Regulation](/en/glossaire/reglement-europeen-essais-cliniques).

*Reference: French and European clinical trial regulation.*
