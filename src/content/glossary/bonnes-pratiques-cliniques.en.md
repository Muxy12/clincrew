Good Clinical Practice (GCP), known in French as Bonnes Pratiques Cliniques (BPC), is the international standard that governs the design, conduct, follow-up and reporting of clinical trials. Its reference is the ICH E6 guideline. GCP pursues two inseparable goals: protecting the rights, safety and well-being of participants, and ensuring the credibility of the data.

## What GCP covers

- The responsibilities of the sponsor, the investigator and the parties involved
- Informed consent and the protection of participants
- The quality, traceability and integrity of data
- Monitoring, archiving and deviation management

## Why it is the CRA's reference

All [clinical monitoring](/en/glossaire/monitoring-clinique) work rests on GCP: a CRA ensures its strict application, from [site selection](/en/services/visite-de-selection) to close-out. It is also the framework checked during an [audit or inspection](/en/glossaire/audit-et-inspection).

*Reference: ICH E6 (Good Clinical Practice).*
