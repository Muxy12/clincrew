The monitoring visit report is the write-up produced by the CRA after each visit to an investigator site. It documents the activities carried out, the findings, any deviations, the corrective actions and their follow-up. It is the written record of monitoring and a key part of the study file.

## What a visit report contains

- The activities carried out during the visit
- The findings and deviations noted (protocol, data, documentation)
- The corrective actions, their owners and their deadlines
- The open items to track until the next visit

## Why it matters

A good report does not just describe the visit: it triggers and tracks actions. It is the follow-up of open items between visits that makes the difference, not the visit itself. The findings also feed the follow-up letter sent to the site.
