Pharmacokinetics studies what happens to a medicine in the body: how it is absorbed, distributed, metabolised and then eliminated (the ADME scheme). It helps to understand how the medicine's concentrations change over time and to adjust doses.

## The four stages (ADME)

- **Absorption**: the medicine entering the body
- **Distribution**: spread through the tissues
- **Metabolism**: transformation by the body
- **Elimination**: excretion out of the body

## Pharmacokinetics and phases

Pharmacokinetic studies are central in [phase I](/en/glossaire/phases-essai-clinique), during the first administrations in humans. They inform the choice of doses for the following phases.
