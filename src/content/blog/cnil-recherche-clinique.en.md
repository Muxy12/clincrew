Clinical research handles health data, among the most sensitive there is. In France, its protection falls under the CNIL (the data protection authority) and the GDPR. Understanding this framework is essential to run a trial by the rules.

## The CNIL's role

The CNIL ensures respect for privacy and the protection of personal data. In clinical research, it frames how participants' data is collected, processed, stored and secured.

## The reference methodologies (MR)

To simplify compliance, the CNIL has defined reference methodologies (notably MR-001 for interventional research and MR-004 for certain non-medicine research). A sponsor that commits to following them benefits from a lighter framework.

## The key principles

- Minimisation: collect only the data needed
- Pseudonymisation of participants' data
- Informing people about the use of their data
- Security and traceability of processing

## CNIL and monitoring

Respect for data confidentiality is checked continuously. During [monitoring visits](/en/services/visites-de-monitoring), the CRA ensures that source data stays protected and that only authorised data is transferred. It is inseparable from [Good Clinical Practice](/en/glossaire/bonnes-pratiques-cliniques).

For the overall regulatory framework, see [clinical trial regulation](/en/blog/reglementation-des-essais-cliniques).

*Reference: GDPR; CNIL reference methodologies.*
