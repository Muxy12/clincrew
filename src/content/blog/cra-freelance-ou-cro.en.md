To run a clinical trial, a sponsor can outsource monitoring to a CRO or call on a freelance CRA. The two options meet different needs in terms of scope, cost and flexibility.

## The CRO: broad coverage

A [CRO](/en/glossaire/cro) can take on an entire study: monitoring, data management, regulatory, biostatistics. This suits large studies, or when the sponsor has no internal team and wants to delegate broadly.

## The freelance CRA: targeted backup

A freelance CRA works on a precise scope: a batch of sites, a few visits, a replacement, a workload peak. They integrate into your team, use your procedures and report in your format, without a permanent structure commitment.

## When to choose each

- **CRO**: a whole study to outsource, a need for multiple services.
- **Freelance CRA**: occasional [monitoring backup](/en/solutions/cra-freelance-cro), flexibility, responsiveness, cost matched to actual activity.

The two are not mutually exclusive: a CRO often reinforces its own teams with freelance CRAs.

## In short

A CRO outsources broadly; a freelance CRA targets and adds flexibility. For a precise monitoring need, the freelance is often the simplest answer.
