A freelance CRA's rate is expressed as a daily rate, billed per day worked. Understanding what it covers helps you compare an independent consultant with a permanent hire.

## What is a freelance CRA's daily rate?

In France, freelance CRA daily rates generally range from €400 to €650 per day, depending on experience, therapeutic area and the complexity of the assignment. That is a market range: the actual rate is confirmed in a tailored proposal.

## What drives the rate

- Level of expertise and years of experience.
- Therapeutic area and protocol complexity (oncology, early phases, innovative therapies).
- Mission length and number of days committed.
- Number of sites, travel and location.
- Urgency and start date.

## Employed vs freelance: the real comparison

A freelance daily rate looks higher than an employee's daily cost, but it includes no employer contributions, paid leave, training, recruitment fees or downtime between studies. You turn a fixed cost into a variable one, calibrated to the real need of the study.

## Why a tailored quote

Every study differs: phase, number of sites, timeline, coverage area. A quote lets you match scope and days to your need, rather than applying a single flat rate. That is the approach I prefer.

- The daily rate is billed per day and depends on several factors.
- The freelance model avoids the fixed costs of hiring.
