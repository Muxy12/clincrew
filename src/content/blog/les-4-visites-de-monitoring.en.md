The monitoring of a clinical trial follows a precise cycle, site by site. From the activation of an investigator site to its closure, the Clinical Research Associate (CRA) carries out four key visits, each with a distinct purpose. Understanding them means understanding the whole course of [clinical monitoring](/en/glossaire/monitoring-clinique).

## 1. The selection visit: assess before opening

Before enrolling a single patient, you verify that a site is genuinely able to run the study: team, patient pool, equipment, protocol compliance. [The selection visit](/en/services/visite-de-selection) leads to a clear decision: keep the site, or not.

## 2. The initiation visit: prepare the start

The site is selected, now it must be prepared. [The initiation visit](/en/services/visite-d-initiation), also called set-up, trains the investigator team on the protocol and procedures, then checks the documentation, the materials and the circuits. Afterwards, the site can start enrolment.

## 3. The monitoring visits: follow up during the study

This is the heart of the follow-up. During enrolment, [the monitoring visits](/en/services/visites-de-monitoring) verify patient safety, protocol compliance and data quality, notably through [source data verification (SDV)](/en/glossaire/sdv). Each visit produces a [monitoring visit report](/en/glossaire/rapport-de-monitoring) listing the findings and the actions to follow.

## 4. The close-out visit: close cleanly

When the study ends at a site, [the close-out visit](/en/services/visite-de-cloture) confirms everything is in order: data resolved, documentation complete, investigational product reconciled, archiving ready. No item should remain open.

## The common thread: the monitoring plan

These four visits are not improvised. They follow [a monitoring plan](/en/glossaire/plan-de-monitoring) defined upfront, increasingly risk-based. And between on-site visits, [remote monitoring](/en/services/monitoring-a-distance) ensures continuity of follow-up.

## In short

- **Selection**: assess the site's capability before opening.
- **Initiation**: prepare and train the team before enrolment.
- **Monitoring**: verify safety, compliance and data.
- **Close-out**: close the site with no open item.
