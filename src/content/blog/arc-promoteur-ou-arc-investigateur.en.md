The term "CRA" actually covers several jobs. The two most often confused are the sponsor CRA and the site CRA. They share the same Good Clinical Practice culture, but do not hold the same position in a clinical trial.

## The sponsor CRA: monitoring from outside

The [sponsor CRA](/en/glossaire/arc-promoteur) works on behalf of the sponsor or the CRO. They carry out the monitoring visits at sites (selection, initiation, follow-up, close-out), verify data and compliance, and act as the link with the project team. This is the profile of a [freelance CRA](/en/services) providing backup.

## The site CRA: working at the site

The [site CRA](/en/glossaire/arc-investigateur) belongs to the site. They organise the study locally: preparing visits, entering data, managing documents. They are the counterpart of the sponsor CRA during visits.

## And the technician?

The [clinical trial technician](/en/glossaire/tec) assists the investigator at the site, like the site CRA, with logistics and data collection.

## Sponsor or site: the essentials

- **Sponsor CRA**: external, monitors, for the sponsor or CRO.
- **Site CRA / technician**: internal to the site, runs the study day to day.

The two collaborate closely during [monitoring visits](/en/services/visites-de-monitoring).

## In short

One oversees from outside, the other runs the study at the site. To reinforce your monitoring teams, you need a sponsor CRA, often freelance.
