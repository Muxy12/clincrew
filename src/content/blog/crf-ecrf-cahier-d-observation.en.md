The CRF (Case Report Form) is the document in which each participant's data in a clinical trial is collected, as specified by the protocol. Today it is most often electronic: this is called the eCRF.

## What the case report form is for

The CRF structures data collection: for each visit, it defines which information to record (endpoints, adverse events, treatments, etc.). It is the raw material for the study's statistical analysis.

## From paper to electronic (eCRF)

The eCRF, integrated into an EDC (Electronic Data Capture) system, has largely replaced paper. It brings automatic consistency checks, remote access and reinforced traceability. It also makes [query](/en/blog/queries-recherche-clinique) handling easier.

## The CRA's role

The CRA does not enter the data (that is the site's role) but verifies it. During monitoring, the CRA checks the agreement between the case report form and the source documents: this is [source data verification (SDV)](/en/glossaire/sdv). Any inconsistency triggers a query.

## eCRF, EDC, CTMS, eTMF

The case report form is part of an ecosystem of tools: the eCRF/EDC for data, the [CTMS](/en/blog/ctms-recherche-clinique) to steer the study, the eTMF for documentation. The [data manager](/en/glossaire/data-manager) is the guarantor on the data side.
