Preparing for a regulatory audit or inspection (ANSM, EMA, FDA) is not a last-minute task. An inspection-ready site is built at every visit, through simple, consistent habits.

## Habits to keep continuously

1. **Consent**: current versions, dated and signed before any procedure.
2. **Source data**: traceable, consistent with the eCRF, corrections documented.
3. **Deviations**: identified, documented and followed through to CAPA.
4. **Investigational product**: accountability, temperature and destruction tracked.
5. **ISF / TMF**: kept in real time, not reconstructed after the fact.

## Before the audit or inspection

A documentation review, a site readiness check and mock questions help anticipate sensitive points. The goal: never having to "catch up" on a file.

## Afterwards: action follow-up

Findings lead to an action plan (CAPA) whose follow-up, to resolution, is part of [audit readiness](/en/services). A deviation closed cleanly builds confidence for what comes next.

## In short

- Readiness is built visit after visit, not the night before.
- Documentation, source data and deviations are the key points.
- Action-plan follow-up closes the loop.
