The CTMS (Clinical Trial Management System) is the software used to steer a clinical trial. Where the [eCRF](/en/blog/crf-ecrf-cahier-d-observation) manages participants' data, the CTMS manages the study itself: sites, visits, recruitment, deadlines and action tracking.

## What a CTMS helps steer

- Follow-up of [investigator sites](/en/glossaire/investigateur) and their status
- Planning and tracking of [monitoring visits](/en/services/visites-de-monitoring)
- Recruitment and enrolment, site by site
- Open actions, documents and deadlines

It is the dashboard that gives an overall view of the study's progress.

## Its value

On a [multicentre](/en/blog/etude-monocentrique-ou-multicentrique) study, manually following dozens of sites quickly becomes unmanageable. The CTMS centralises information, avoids oversights and makes reporting to the [sponsor](/en/glossaire/promoteur) easier.

## CTMS and the tool ecosystem

The CTMS does not work alone: it connects with the eCRF/EDC (data), the eTMF (essential documentation) and the [monitoring plan](/en/glossaire/plan-de-monitoring) tools. A CRA comfortable with these systems gains efficiency.

## The CRA's role

The CRA feeds the CTMS: visit reports, open items, site status. Up-to-date data in the CTMS means reliable steering of the study.
