Data quality is the whole point of monitoring. Two levers secure it day to day: source data verification (SDV / SDR) and rigorous query management.

## SDV and SDR: what are they?

Source Data Verification (SDV) compares the data entered in the eCRF with the site's source documents. Source Data Review (SDR) looks more broadly at the consistency and plausibility of the data, beyond a simple comparison.

## Query management

When an inconsistency appears, a query is raised and then followed to resolution. Fast, documented handling prevents a backlog and keeps the database clean and ready for analysis. [Monitoring visits](/en/services/visites-de-monitoring) are the key moment for this control.

## Data integrity

Beyond accuracy, integrity rests on traceability: who entered what, when, and which corrections were made. The ALCOA principles (attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original, accurate) frame this requirement.

## In short

- SDV compares eCRF and source data; SDR assesses their consistency.
- Queries are handled quickly and documented.
- Traceability (ALCOA) safeguards data integrity.
