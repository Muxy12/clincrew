Hiring a freelance CRA for your study is faster than a permanent hire: a few steps are enough to secure the oversight of your sites. Here is how to proceed.

## 1. Define the need

Specify the therapeutic area, study phase, number of sites, timeline and current challenges. This initial scoping lets you assess the volume of days and the scope of the assignment.

## 2. Where to find a freelance CRA

You can contact an independent consultant directly (through their website or LinkedIn) or use a specialist platform. Check experience in monitoring, the therapeutic areas covered and command of GCP / ICH-GCP.

## 3. The contracting process

After a first discussion and agreement on scope, a service agreement is signed. The assignment can then begin within days. Together you define the sites involved, the types of [visits](/en/services), the frequency and the deliverables (visit reports, follow-up letters, dashboards).

## 4. What to check

- Up-to-date GCP / ICH-GCP training.
- A valid legal status and professional liability insurance.
- Confidentiality and GDPR compliance.
- Ability to follow your procedures (SOPs) and tools.

## 5. Getting started

After onboarding onto your tools and procedures, the CRA coordinates with your internal teams, vendors and sites. Regular sync points keep everyone aligned.

## In short

- Scope the need, then validate expertise and compliance.
- A service agreement is enough to start quickly.
- Check GCP, insurance, confidentiality and tool compatibility.
