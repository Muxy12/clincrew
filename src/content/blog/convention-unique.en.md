The single agreement (convention unique) is the contract that frames, in France, industry-sponsored clinical trials run in healthcare facilities. Introduced to simplify and speed up contracting, it replaced the many separate agreements that existed before.

## What it is for

Before the single agreement, each trial could lead to lengthy negotiations, site by site. The goal: a harmonised contract template between the industrial [sponsor](/en/glossaire/promoteur) and the facility, with a standardised cost grid, to reduce set-up times.

## What it frames

- The respective commitments of the sponsor and the facility
- The financial counterparts (research-related extra costs)
- The practical terms for running the study

## Its impact on set-up

Shorter contracting times mean faster site start-up. Set-up time is often a bottleneck for trials. The single agreement therefore acts directly on how quickly [investigator sites](/en/glossaire/investigateur) open.

## Single agreement and monitoring

Once the agreement is signed and the site opens, it is time for the [initiation visit](/en/services/visite-d-initiation) and then follow-up. The CRA does not handle the agreement, but works within the framework it sets.
