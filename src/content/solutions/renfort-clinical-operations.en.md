A Clinical Operations team runs as long as its resources keep up. A departure, an extended absence or a workload peak can be enough to put site follow-up under strain. A freelance CRA brings targeted operational backup to keep activity going, for as long as needed.

## What the backup brings

- Hand-over of sites left without an assigned CRA
- Continuity of monitoring and open-item follow-up
- Keeping the pace of visits planned in the monitoring plan
- Deliverables defined with your Clinical Operations team

## Taking over sites without losing information

The hand-over starts with a review of what exists: latest visit reports, open items, documentation and timeline. I then plan contact with each site concerned and continue the follow-up according to the monitoring plan, in line with Good Clinical Practice (ICH E6).

## Backup sized to your need

Scope and duration are set with you: a few sites over a few weeks, or broader backup over several months. The goal is to get through the gap without a break in follow-up.
