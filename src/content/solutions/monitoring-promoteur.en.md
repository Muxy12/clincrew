Some sponsors oversee their sites themselves but need reliable support to carry out the monitoring activities. A freelance CRA takes on these activities on your behalf, within the agreed scope and responsibilities, while you keep oversight of the study.

## What I carry out on your behalf

- On-site visits: selection, initiation, monitoring, close-out
- Verification of data, informed consent and investigational product management
- Follow-up of deviations and corrective actions
- Reporting for your team, in the agreed format

## A clear split of responsibilities

Delegated responsibilities are formalised before the assignment: activities entrusted, system access, applicable procedures, deliverables and communication channels. The sponsor keeps responsibility for the study and the decisions; I carry out the monitoring activities within that framework, in line with Good Clinical Practice (ICH E6).

## To complement an internal team

This solution suits sponsors that already have a team but want external backup on some sites or visits, without expanding their permanent headcount.
