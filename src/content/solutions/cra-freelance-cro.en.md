When a CRO's monitoring workload exceeds its capacity, hiring takes time the study does not always have. A freelance CRA brings immediate extra capacity, on a defined assignment, without adding to your structure. You keep control of the project; I absorb the workload on the agreed scope.

## What the backup covers

- A batch of sites to follow, or a few targeted visits (selection, initiation, monitoring, close-out)
- Data review and query follow-up in your EDC
- Tracking of open items and corrective actions between visits
- Reporting in your format, at the frequency you set

## How I fit into your project team

I work with your procedures, your tools (EDC, CTMS, eTMF) and your communication channels. I coordinate with the project manager and the sites, and report like a member of the team, in line with Good Clinical Practice (ICH E6). Scope, responsibilities and deliverables are set before the start.

## When to call on it

A workload peak, a study starting before the team is complete, an absence to cover, or a batch of sites to take over: situations where occasional backup avoids hiring in a hurry.
