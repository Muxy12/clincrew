Many biotechs run a clinical trial without a permanent monitoring team. Rather than building an internal CRA team for a single study, you outsource the monitoring activities to a freelance CRA. You keep control of strategy and decisions; I take care of the operational follow-up of the sites you entrust to me.

## What you can outsource

- Site set-up: selection and initiation
- Monitoring visits and data follow-up
- Remote monitoring between on-site visits
- Site close-out and documentation checks

You outsource a whole study or only certain activities, depending on the scope defined with you.

## You keep responsibility for your study

Outsourcing monitoring does not mean delegating strategy. You remain the sponsor, responsible for decisions and oversight. I work within the framework you set, following your procedures and Good Clinical Practice (ICH E6), and report the information relevant to your steering.

## A resource mobilised for the study

You access experienced operational capacity, mobilised for the time required, without permanent structure cost or recruitment.
